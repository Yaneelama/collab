Who we are

Run by guides,
not a booking engine.

Himalaya Trails was founded by three Sherpa and Gurung guides who were tired of watching foreign agencies take the margins on routes they'd walked since childhood. Every itinerary on this site is built and led in-house — no subcontracted crews, no surprise costs at altitude.
